What owners ask first

Fair questions. Straight answers.

“AI will make things up.”

Every reply quotes the policy line it stands on. If no line covers the ticket, we don't answer it. It goes to your team with a note.

“Will it know our products?”

In week one we write your policy with your best agent, including what only they know. You approve every line before a single reply goes out.

“Will it sound like us?”

It answers from your policy, in your words, from your address. Your 10 free tickets show you before you pay anything.

“Will we have to redo its work?”

Every week you see how many replies a person had to change. A person reads every reply for the first 30 days. Stop the pilot any day with one day's notice.

“What happens in December?”

Same price per ticket. No holiday hires to find and train, and no ramp-up while the volume climbs.

“What if our support person quits?”

Nothing changes for your customers. There's no one to rehire and no one to retrain. You don't set anything up: we take an agent seat in your helpdesk, like a new hire.

Questions

Before you send tickets

Is this a shared bot that answers for every brand?

No. It answers only from your policy, in your words, from your address. Your tickets and replies are used only for your own service and are never shared with another client.

Which helpdesks do you work with?

Gorgias, Zendesk, Help Scout, Richpanel, Commslayer and others. We take the same agent seat a new hire gets. No helpdesk? We work straight from your support inbox.

What counts as a resolved ticket?

A reply we sent with no customer complaint within 7 days. You only pay the full rate for those.

What always goes to my team?

Anything your policy doesn't cover, plus anything you put on your always-escalate list, like chargebacks or refunds over a set amount. Each one comes with a one-line note.

What do I sign?

A one-page, 30-day pilot. Either side can stop it with one day's notice. If you keep going, it becomes a 12-month term at the same price, and prices don't rise during it.

Who will I talk to?

The founder, directly. No account managers.
